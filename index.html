01 / ABOUT

One career, connected
by better decisions.

The common thread in my work is simple: make complex information useful, and make the result clear enough to act on.

I began in civil engineering and project controls, where plans, cost, and uncertainty affect real projects. That experience taught me to start with the decision a team needs to make—not the tool that happens to be available.

I carried that habit into retail analytics, using forecasts, customer analysis, and computer vision to help teams work with large product catalogs. Today, I build applied AI systems that turn documents and data into evidence-linked, reviewable outputs.

Across each domain, I care about the same things: clear assumptions, useful measures, human review where it matters, and systems that can be operated after the prototype.
